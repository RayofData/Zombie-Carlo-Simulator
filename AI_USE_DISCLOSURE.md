## Project-Specific Disclosure

> AI was used as an advisory tool for project planning, focused code examples, debugging, documentation, and refinement of the Streamlit interface and Monte Carlo simulation.
>
> The author designed and implemented the core project, reviewed and modified AI suggestions before incorporating them, and manually evaluated the simulation behavior and outputs. AI was not relied upon as the primary source of verification; a focused automated test suite is planned as a subsequent project step.
